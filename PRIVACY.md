# Privacy

OmniVisuals is a local shader, texture, and preset package for OmniShade. It does not add an OmniVex telemetry, analytics, advertising, account, or cloud service.

Shader configuration and preset data remain on the local computer. OmniVisuals does not upload gameplay captures, frame data, depth buffers, settings, or diagnostic information.

The game, launcher, graphics driver, OmniShade runtime, overlays, GitHub, Patreon, Ko-fi, and email are separate components or services with their own behavior and privacy terms.

Before requesting support, remove usernames, game-library paths, account identifiers, and unrelated information from screenshots or logs.
